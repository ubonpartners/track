**Decision**: keep v14 in production. v15 ships in `bench/data/` as a
validation artefact and a starting point for further tuning (the gain
on trace-lib is the biggest single-recipe move we've seen).

Same architecture (TwoTower with face/subbox features auto-detected

Apples-to-apples re-bench (both heads run forward on the SAME

| Head                   | Aggregator | Val AUC | Δ over current pre-thr baseline |
|------------------------|------------|---------|---------------------------------|
| pre_thr_score (no NN)  | —          | 0.9650  | —                               |
| v9 (shipped)           | ema_fixed  | 0.9663  | +0.0013                         |
| **v10 (this retrain)** | ema_fixed  | 0.9742  | **+0.0092**                     |

(The 0.9950 number on `phase3_v9_face.pt['best_va_auc']` was on the

+0.0013, not +0.03.)

The reason v9's delta is so small here is that the current pair-logs
were generated with v9 already enabled in the runtime — so
`pre_thr_score` in this corpus already contains v9's residual baked in.
v10's +0.0092 measures genuine improvement over a v9-already-applied
baseline, i.e. v10 is meaningfully better than v9.

**Decision**: v10 is a candidate replacement for v9. Before flipping
the YAML pointer, run `eval_head_fitness --subset full --runs 5` so we
have a fitness-not-AUC number, and a CEVO trace check.

## 2026-05-10 evening — second-pass state-head retrain (v20)

Continued search for a fitness win after the v15 fitness regression.
Sequence of experiments and what we learned:

1. **Scene-aggregate features (in_dim=25, "v16-scene")** — the 6 Phase-29
   per-scene EMAs (promote_rate, mean_det_conf_TRACKED/unmatched,
   track_density_smooth, mean_alive_track_age, det_conf_minus_scene_TP_avg)
   were plumbed end-to-end into the C runtime (commit
   `ubon_cstuff:0397799`) and trained as v16-scene + v17 variants
   (h32+w3, h32+w5, h64+w1, h64+w3). Every scene-feature variant lost
   on full-178 fitness (-0.011 to -0.017) because the extra
   discrimination made the head MORE aggressive, doubling fp_tracks.
   c_FP_track sweeps couldn't recover the gap.

2. **`pos_weight < 1` BCE bias (v20)** — the actual win. Telling the
   trainer that false-positives cost more than false-negatives
   (pos_weight < 1) produces a head that's slightly more conservative
   than v14 without losing the capacity gains of hidden=64. Full-178
   3-run results:

   | Head                       | fitness ± σ      | mota    | fp_tr |
   |----------------------------|------------------|---------|------:|
   | v14 (prior ship)           | +0.4759 ± 0.0012 | +0.5175 |  79.0 |
   | v18 h64 pw=1.0             | +0.4651 ± 0.0032 | +0.5292 | 123.7 |
   | v20 h64 pw=0.7             | +0.4778 ± 0.0006 | +0.5218 |  83.7 |
   | **v20 h64 pw=0.5 (SHIP)**  | **+0.4801 ± 0.0013** | +0.5108 | 57.3 |
   | v20 h64 pw=0.4             | +0.4802 ± 0.0009 | +0.5056 |  47.0 |
   | v20 h64 pw=0.3 (1 run)     | +0.4331 (overcooked) | 0.4432 |  17  |

   Best is **pw=0.5 with h=64** at fitness +0.4801 — a clean **+0.0042
   fitness over v14**, ~3σ above run-to-run noise. The fitness curve is
   broadly flat between pw=0.4 and pw=0.5 (both at +0.0042), so pw=0.5
   was picked as the slightly less aggressive in the conservative
   direction (keeps a bit more mota).

3. **What this means** — `pos_weight` was a long-standing trainer arg
   (defaulted to 1.0). The win is just: hidden=64 + pos_weight=0.5.
   No architecture change, no new features, no corpus change.

**Decision**: nn_state_v20_pw05.bin ships in `uc_v11.yaml` +
`uc_v11_fiqa.yaml`. v14_dc moved to `_archive/`. Scene-feature plumbing
stays in the C runtime — it's a strict superset (in_dim={19,25}) so
v14 / v20 (both in_dim=19) load as before, and the path is open if a
future training recipe makes scene features genuinely pay.
